Who We Are

The Page Turners is a warm and welcoming book club based in Tucson, Arizona. We've been meeting monthly since 2018, bringing together readers who love fiction, history, memoir, and everything in between.

Each month, one of our members selects and facilitates discussion of a chosen book. Our meetings are lively, thoughtful, and always a little bit fun. We welcome readers of all backgrounds and encourage members to suggest their own reading picks.

Meetings are held on the second Thursday of each month from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM. We alternate between meeting in person at the Tucson Public Library and meeting virtually via Zoom so that all members can join comfortably.
